# Credit memo (draft): Ashgrove Bakery Co Ltd

> Draft prepared from the borrower's source documents. Every figure is a link to its source snippet or calculation (see the appendix). The recommendation is reserved for the credit officer.

## Borrower overview

Ashgrove Bakery Co Ltd (wholesale bakery supplying retail chains) has applied for an amortising term loan of [£600k](#ref-ashgrove.app.amount). Stated purpose: Working capital and oven line refurbishment. The facility would run for [48 months](#ref-ashgrove.app.term) at an indicative rate of [9.00%](#ref-ashgrove.app.rate), repaid in equal monthly instalments. Security offered: Debenture over company assets and a director guarantee, valued by the applicant at [£450k](#ref-ashgrove.app.collateral_value), which is [0.75x](#ref-ashgrove.FY2023.collateral_cover) cover of the requested amount (applicant's valuation, not independently verified).

Revenue for FY2023 was [£5,700k](#ref-ashgrove.FY2023.2023.revenue) (growth of [-5.8%](#ref-ashgrove.FY2023.revenue_growth) on FY2022), with EBITDA of [£494k](#ref-ashgrove.FY2023.ebitda) and net income of [£33k](#ref-ashgrove.FY2023.2023.net_income). The analysis uses the borrower's annual financial statements for FY2021 to FY2023, extracted from the supplied documents; every figure below links to its source.

Pro forma, existing debt service of [£430k](#ref-ashgrove.FY2023.debt_service) plus new-loan debt service of [£179k](#ref-ashgrove.FY2023.new_debt_service) gives a DSCR of [0.79x](#ref-ashgrove.FY2023.dscr_pro_forma).
