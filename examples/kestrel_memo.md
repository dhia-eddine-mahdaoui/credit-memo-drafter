# Credit memo (draft): Kestrel Bay Logistics Ltd

> Draft prepared from the borrower's source documents. Every figure is a link to its source snippet or calculation (see the appendix). The recommendation is reserved for the credit officer.

## Borrower overview

Kestrel Bay Logistics Ltd (regional road freight and warehousing) has applied for an amortising term loan of [£2,500k](#ref-kestrel.app.amount). Stated purpose: Fleet expansion: refrigerated delivery vehicles. The facility would run for [84 months](#ref-kestrel.app.term) at an indicative rate of [8.50%](#ref-kestrel.app.rate), repaid in equal monthly instalments. Security offered: Charge over the financed vehicles and a floating charge, valued by the applicant at [£2,900k](#ref-kestrel.app.collateral_value), which is [1.16x](#ref-kestrel.FY2023.collateral_cover) cover of the requested amount (applicant's valuation, not independently verified).

Revenue for FY2023 was [£19,100k](#ref-kestrel.FY2023.2023.revenue) (growth of [6.0%](#ref-kestrel.FY2023.revenue_growth) on FY2022), with EBITDA of [£2,092k](#ref-kestrel.FY2023.ebitda) and net income of [£204k](#ref-kestrel.FY2023.2023.net_income). The analysis uses the borrower's annual financial statements for FY2021 to FY2023, extracted from the supplied documents; every figure below links to its source.
