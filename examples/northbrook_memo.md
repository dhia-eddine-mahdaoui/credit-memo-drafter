# Credit memo (draft): Northbrook Precision Tooling Ltd

> Draft prepared from the borrower's source documents. Every figure is a link to its source snippet or calculation (see the appendix). The recommendation is reserved for the credit officer.

## Borrower overview

Northbrook Precision Tooling Ltd (precision machined components for industrial customers) has applied for an amortising term loan of [£1,800k](#ref-northbrook.app.amount). Stated purpose: Purchase of a five-axis CNC machining centre. The facility would run for [60 months](#ref-northbrook.app.term) at an indicative rate of [7.25%](#ref-northbrook.app.rate), repaid in equal monthly instalments. Security offered: First charge over the financed machine and the freehold workshop, valued by the applicant at [£2,600k](#ref-northbrook.app.collateral_value), which is [1.44x](#ref-northbrook.FY2023.collateral_cover) cover of the requested amount (applicant's valuation, not independently verified).

Revenue for FY2023 was [£12,100k](#ref-northbrook.FY2023.2023.revenue) (growth of [7.1%](#ref-northbrook.FY2023.revenue_growth) on FY2022), with EBITDA of [£2,177k](#ref-northbrook.FY2023.ebitda) and net income of [£1,074k](#ref-northbrook.FY2023.2023.net_income). The analysis uses the borrower's annual financial statements for FY2021 to FY2023, extracted from the supplied documents; every figure below links to its source.
